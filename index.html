About

Hi there! I'm Diba — an education researcher and trainee teacher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

I'm currently pursuing my M.Ed. in Educational Evaluation and Research at IER, University of Dhaka, after completing my B.Ed. in Language Education with a CGPA of 3.87.

Over the past few years I've worked across qualitative and quantitative research — from field interviews on sensitive topics to structured surveys across hundreds of teachers — while also teaching Grades 6–10 directly.

My focus now is simple: help close the gap between what research says works in classrooms, and what actually happens in them.
